# Brand marks

Toyota, Mercedes-Benz, BMW, Hyundai, Kia, Mazda, Nissan and Renault PNGs are unmodified recognizable brand marks obtained from:
https://github.com/filippofilip95/car-logos-dataset/tree/master/logos/optimized

Used solely to identify vehicles linked to independent assistance guides. Trademarks remain the property of their respective owners; display does not imply affiliation, authorization or official dealership status. These are third-party supplied marks, not AI-generated or hand-drawn substitutes.
